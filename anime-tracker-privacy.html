Anime Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026
Short version: Anime Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user profiles, and no telemetry. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours for it to be sent to.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally so it can work offline and remember your preferences. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Favorites — which shows you starred, so the app can notify you before a new episode airs.
  • Cached schedule — the most recently loaded airing schedule, so the app opens instantly and still works without a connection.
  • Settings — such as which browser you chose for opening links.

Third-party services

To show airing times, artwork, and where each series is available, the app requests publicly available anime data from:

  • AniList (API docs)
  • Jikan, a community API for MyAnimeList data

These requests contain only the schedule query itself — no account information and no identifiers, because the app has none to send. As with any internet request, those services receive your device's IP address; their own privacy policies govern that. Cover artwork is loaded directly from AniList's image servers and remains the property of its respective copyright holders.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app periodically checks the public airing schedule for the shows you starred and posts a reminder shortly before a new episode airs. This check runs entirely on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's system settings.

Links to other apps and sites

Tapping a streaming platform opens that service in your browser or its own app. The app only ever links to official, licensed streaming platforms. Once you leave, that service's privacy policy applies.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "last updated" date.
